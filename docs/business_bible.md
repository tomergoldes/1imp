## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1IMP is not a resume builder. It is not a video platform. It is not a profile creator.

**1IMP is the infrastructure layer that determines who gets hired.**

The PDF resume is a 30-year-old information format being used to make decisions worth hundreds of thousands of dollars. The entire $750B global recruitment industry runs on an input format that predates the internet. This is not a niche problem. This is a civilizational inefficiency.

Our model: **Win the candidate first. Quietly occupy the recruiter's workflow second. Become the standard third.**

This is the playbook Workday ran on HR software. It is the playbook Stripe ran on payments. It is the playbook we will run on talent.

## PART 1 — CUSTOMER SEGMENTS

### The Honest Hierarchy

Most companies lie about who their customer is. We will not.

| Priority | Segment | Role | Why |
|----------|---------|------|-----|
| 1 | **Active Job Seeker** | Primary user + payer | They feel the pain acutely. They are motivated to pay. They are the viral vector. |
| 2 | **Passive Job Seeker** | Long-term sleeper | Large market. Low urgency today. High urgency tomorrow. Build habit now. |
| 3 | **Career Changer** | Premium buyer | Higher anxiety = higher willingness to pay. Will pay for one-time package. |
| 4 | **Recent Graduate** | Volume play | Low ARPU but massive volume. Best word-of-mouth. |
| 5 | **Executive / Senior Leader** | Luxury segment | Low volume, extremely high LTV. White-glove tier justified. |
| 6 | **Freelancer / Consultant** | Underserved use case | Profile doubles as a client-facing portfolio. Natural upsell. |
| 7 | **Recruiter (Individual)** | Secondary user | Our viral loop depends on them. Do not charge them early. Make them addicted first. |
| 8 | **HR Team / Talent Acquisition** | B2B buyer | Year 2-3 revenue. Not the MVP focus. |
| 9 | **University Career Centers** | B2B2C distribution** | Cheap distribution to thousands of students. Partnership model. |
| 10 | **Enterprise (CHRO buyer)** | Year 4+ play | The ATS disruption. Long sales cycle. Massive ACV. |

### Why Candidate-First is Non-Negotiable

> "You cannot build a two-sided marketplace by starting with the supply side."

Recruiters only care about 1IMP if candidates are already there. Candidates only stay if recruiters respond. This is the classic cold-start problem.

**Our answer**: Make the candidate product so good that candidates flood the platform. Make viewing a profile so frictionless (no login, instant load, mobile-perfect) that recruiters consume it without thinking. The two-sided flywheel starts with one side moving first.

**Challenge to assumption**: One competing argument is "Start with recruiters — they control budget." This is wrong for 1IMP. Why? Because if we build for recruiters first, we become an ATS — a B2B enterprise sales product with 9-month sales cycles and 0 viral potential. The consumer brand is our moat. LinkedIn took 10 years to build it. We must protect it from day one.

## PART 2 — REVENUE MODEL

### The Master Architecture

```
REVENUE STREAMS (ordered by timeline, not by importance)

  Year 1 ─── B2C Freemium (Pro subscription)
           ─── One-Time Purchase (Impression Pack)
  Year 2 ─── B2C Elite Tier
           ─── University Partnerships
  Year 3 ─── Recruiter Pro (Lightweight, self-serve)
           ─── B2B Teams Starter
  Year 4 ─── Enterprise ATS (Full platform)
           ─── Data Licensing (anonymized insights)
  Year 5 ─── The Talent Marketplace (candidate database)
```

### Why This Ordering is Correct

Starting with enterprise = wrong. Enterprise has 9-month sales cycles, requires SOC2, demands legal reviews, and cannot fund the engineering needed to also build a beautiful consumer product. You end up with an ugly tool that no one loves.

Starting with freemium consumer = correct. Freemium drives organic distribution. Every free user is a salesperson. Every share is an impression on a recruiter. The consumer base becomes the demand that justifies the enterprise pitch in Year 3.

### The Revenue Philosophy

> **Rule**: Never charge a candidate for the core ability to get hired.
> **Rule**: Charge for insights, customization, speed, and power.
> **Rule**: Recruiter tools must feel like a gift before they feel like a product.

## PART 3 — FREE PLAN (THE DISTRIBUTION ENGINE)

### Design Philosophy

The free plan is not charity. It is our most important marketing channel.

Every free profile is a permanent advertisement served directly to a decision-maker (recruiter). The "Powered by 1IMP" badge on every free profile converts recruiters into users. This is Dropbox's referral loop. It is Calendly's link-in-bio loop. It is Notion's shared-page loop. The product distributes itself through its own output.

### Free Tier — Complete Feature Set

| Feature | Why It's Free | Strategic Purpose |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| 1 public impression profile | Core value delivery | Can't convert what you haven't shown |
| AI professional summary | The Aha Moment | Without this, we have no product |
| 1 AI career pitch (video script) | Taste of premium | Creates desire to upgrade for more |
| Public profile page (yourname.1imp.io) | Viral distribution | Every URL is a marketing URL |
| Shareable link + QR code | Frictionless sharing | The share IS the growth loop |
| Basic view count | Dopamine hook | "Someone viewed me" → upgrade intent |
| PDF resume download | ATS compatibility | Removes recruiter objection |
| "Powered by 1IMP" viral footer | Our billboard | Non-negotiable. This is the business model. |
| 3 basic themes | Quality signal | Free must look premium or brand suffers |

### The Viral Coefficient Logic

If 1 candidate shares their 1IMP link, and the recruiter who views it shares 1IMP with 2 colleagues, and 1 of those colleagues creates their own profile → K-factor = 1.3.

K-factor > 1.0 = viral. The free plan must be designed to maximize K.

**Key insight**: The free plan is optimized for SHARING, not for COMPLETION. A profile that is 80% complete and shared beats a 100% complete profile that is never shared.

## PART 4 — ONE-TIME PURCHASE (THE CAREER PACK)

### Why This Tier Must Exist

**The uncomfortable truth about job seekers**: Many are unemployed. They are cash-conscious. They are skeptical of subscriptions they might "forget to cancel." Monthly recurring feels like a trap when you don't know if you'll find a job in 2 weeks or 6 months.

A one-time purchase removes all psychological friction. "Pay once. Use forever." This framing converts people who would never subscribe.

This is not a compromise. It is a psychologically-aware revenue design.

### 1IMP Career Pack — $39 One-Time

| Included Feature | Why |
|-----------------|-----|
| 3 impression profiles | Apply to 3 different role types (PM, Designer, Founder) |
| Premium AI summary + full rewrite | Higher quality output than free generation |

### Pricing Rationale: $39

- Below the psychological ceiling of $49 ("almost $50")

- Comparable to a professional headshot ($100-200) or resume review service ($99-299)
- Dramatically below a career coach session ($150-400)
- Feels proportionate to a "one-time career investment"

**Upsell path**: After purchase, at the 30-day mark, show: "Your Career Pack company viewer access expires in 3 days. Upgrade to Pro to keep seeing who's looking at you."

## PART 6 — RECRUITER PLATFORM

### Design Philosophy

> "Recruiters should never be forced to watch hundreds of videos. That is not a recruiter problem. That is a candidate problem being solved by adding friction."

The recruiter experience must be built around **speed to signal**. In 10 seconds, a recruiter must be able to decide: "Worth 3 minutes" or "Pass." Our UI must enforce this.

### The Correct Information Hierarchy (Speed-Optimized)

```
1. ── Photo + Name + Title + Location              (0.5 seconds)
      → Immediate human connection + geography filter

2. ── AI Summary (3-4 sentences)                   (8 seconds)
      → "Who is this person in plain English"

3. ── Candidate Highlights (3 bullet points)        (5 seconds)
      → Biggest achievements, quantified

4. ── Skills Cloud (tagged, categorized)            (3 seconds)
      → Tech stack / domain expertise at a glance

5. ── Experience Timeline (compact)                 (15 seconds)
      → Where, how long, what level

6. ── Video Pitch (optional, one-click play)        (60 seconds)
      → Only for candidates who want to go deeper

7. ── Full Resume Download                          → Only if they want the ATS-formatted doc
```

**Why this order?**

- Recruiters make in/out decisions in **7 seconds**. The visual + summary must capture them.
- Video is last because: not all candidates record one, not all recruiter contexts allow sound, and bad video harms more than it helps. Optional. Never mandatory.
- Resume is last because: the 1IMP format already contains all the same information, structured better. If they've gotten to resume, they want it.

### Recruiter Pricing Strategy

**Phase 1 (Year 1-2): Zero-Cost Passive Consumption**

Recruiters can view any public 1IMP profile for free, forever. No account required. This is the deliberate strategy.

**Why free?** Because forcing recruiters to create an account is the #1 way to kill the candidate's viral loop. The recruiter sees a link in their inbox. If they have to sign up to view it, they close the tab. The impression dies. The candidate gets no callback. The product fails.

**Phase 2 (Year 2-3): Recruiter Pro — $29/month/seat**

Once recruiters are habituated to consuming 1IMP profiles (they will be, because candidates force them there), we introduce light tools:

| Feature | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Save profiles to shortlists | "Bookmark" candidates for later |
| Team sharing | Forward a candidate to a colleague |
| "Invite to 1IMP" | Nudge a candidate who doesn't have a profile |
| Candidate notes | Private notes on each profile |
| Viewed history | Track which profiles you've reviewed |
| Search saved profiles | Filter by skill, role, location |

**Phase 3 (Year 3+): Recruiter Business — $99/month/team**

| Feature | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Team collaboration on shortlists | |
| ATS integration (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday) | Export candidate data |
| Branded recruiter page | "Talent at [Company Name]" |
| Inbound pipeline from candidates | |
| Analytics on recruiter team's activity | |

## PART 7 — ENTERPRISE VISION

### The 5-Year Platform Map

**The thesis**: Enterprise HR is built on top of two broken experiences: candidates submitting PDFs into black holes, and recruiters reading 250 identical documents per role. 1IMP disrupts both simultaneously.

### Year 3-4: 1IMP for Enterprise

| Product | Target Buyer | ACV |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| 1IMP Teams (10-50 users) | Talent Acquisition leads | $15,000-30,000/year |
| 1IMP Enterprise (unlimited seats) | CHRO at 500-5000 person companies | $50,000-150,000/year |
| 1IMP ATS (full replacement) | Companies dissatisfied with Greenhouse/Lever | $100,000-500,000/year |

### Key Enterprise Features

**1. AI Candidate Discovery**
Enterprise customers can define a role and ideal candidate profile. Our AI scans the 1IMP candidate database (with opt-in consent) and surfaces the top 10 matching profiles — similar to LinkedIn Recruiter, but with better data (richer profiles, higher signal, more recent).

**2. Branded Candidate Portal**
Companies can create a "1IMP Career Page" — a beautiful, interactive employer brand page where candidates apply directly with their 1IMP profiles. Replaces static careers pages.

**3. HR Analytics Dashboard**
- Which roles are attracting the best candidates?
- What skills are most common in top-performing new hires?
- Where are candidates dropping out of the funnel?

**4. ATS Integrations**
Day 1 of enterprise: Greenhouse, Lever, Workday export. Year 3: Native 1IMP ATS that replaces them.

**5. University Enterprise Partnerships**
License 1IMP to universities as a career services platform. Every student in the institution gets a Pro account. University pays per-seat annually ($5-15/student/year). University of 20,000 students = $100-300K/year. Target 100 universities = $10-30M ARR from this channel alone.

### Retention Strategy

**The problem**: Job seekers by definition leave when they get a job. This is our retention enemy.

**The solutions**:

2. **Passive job seeker mode**: "I'm employed but open to exceptional opportunities." Profile stays live. User checks analytics occasionally. Retention without active usage.

3. **Career milestone updates**: Birthdays, work anniversaries, promotions → trigger "Update your 1IMP" nudges.

4. **The Portfolio Pivot**: Frame 1IMP not just as a job search tool, but as a permanent professional identity. "Your 1IMP is your professional home page." Users who see it as an identity asset churn 70% less than those who see it as a job search tool.

## PART 9 — GROWTH STRATEGY

### The Four Acquisition Channels (Priority Order)

**Channel 1: Product-Led Viral (Free)**
The shareable link IS the distribution mechanism. Every time a candidate shares their profile, 1IMP is demonstrated to a decision-maker. Design the share moment to be irresistible.

*Target metric*: Time-to-First-Share < 30 minutes from signup. Users who share within 30 minutes retain at 4x the rate of those who don't.

**Channel 2: Influencer Seeding**
20 creators in the career/hiring space get white-glove onboarded. Their 1IMP link goes in every bio. Their audience of 50K-500K career-minded people sees a premium profile and clicks "Create mine."

*Target creators*: Career coaches, "day in the life of a recruiter" TikTok/LinkedIn creators, tech layoff community leaders, college career advisors with big following.

**Channel 3: Community Infiltration**
We don't advertise in communities. We participate. The team posts genuinely helpful content in:
- /r/cscareerquestions (1.2M members)
- /r/jobs, /r/recruitinghell
- Tech Layoffs Discord servers
- Out-of-work Slack communities
- LinkedIn "Open to Work" hashtag ecosystem

*Rule*: Never post "try 1IMP." Post genuinely helpful content. Put the 1IMP link in the profile. Let them discover.

**Channel 4: SEO (Programmatic)**
Every opted-in public profile creates a Google-indexable page:
`1imp.io/p/sara-chen-product-manager-san-francisco`

When a recruiter googles "Product Manager resume San Francisco," our profiles appear. This is Zillow's playbook. Yelp's playbook. Airbnb's playbook. User-generated content as SEO.

```
[ACQUIRE] ─── Candidate signs up (free)
               ↓
```

## STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS (CEO-Level)

### Where Existing Strategy is Correct

1. **Candidate-first philosophy** — Correct. Never compromise this.
2. **Recruiter-free viewing** — Correct. Any friction kills the viral loop.
3. **"Powered by 1IMP" footer** — Correct. This is the business model in a badge.
4. **Aha Moment focus** — Correct. The preview moment must be emotionally powerful.
5. **PLG before enterprise** — Correct. Enterprise legitimacy comes from consumer scale.

### Where Existing Strategy Should be Challenged

**Challenge #1: The $12/month Pro price may be too low for Year 2+**
At $12, we signal "affordable tool." At $19-24, we signal "professional investment." Once we have traction and social proof, increase price. Early adopters get grandfathered. This is Notion's, Linear's, and Figma's playbook.

**Recommendation**: Launch at $12. Raise to $19 at 10K users. Raise to $24 at 50K users.

**Challenge #2: The one-time pack ($39) may cannibalize Pro subscription**
Some users will buy once and never upgrade. This is acceptable in Year 1 for cash flow. In Year 2, the one-time pack should expire (30-day analytics, 90-day premium features) to create re-purchase or subscription conversion moments.

**Recommendation**: Rebrand as "1IMP Career Sprint" — 90-day access for $39. Renewal or upgrade to Pro at day 88.

**Challenge #3: Video is mentioned but de-prioritized**
Video is NOT dead in hiring. In 2025, asynchronous video interviews (Hireflix, Spark Hire) are growing. The question is not whether to include video, but how. Our answer: **AI-coached, one-take video with teleprompter mode.** Not free-form recording. Guided. Polished. One click.

**Recommendation**: Launch video as a Pro feature in V1.5. Not earlier. Quality floor is mandatory.

**Challenge #4: Missing the "employed but open" market**
LinkedIn has the "Open to Opportunities" toggle. We need an equivalent. Passive candidates are 70% of the workforce. If 1IMP only serves active job seekers, we serve 30% of the market.

**Recommendation**: Build a "Professional Home Page" mode. "This is me. Not actively looking. But if the right opportunity found me..." This keeps profiles live forever and turns 1IMP into a LinkedIn competitor for professional identity.
