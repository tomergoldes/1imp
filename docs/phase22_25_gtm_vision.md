## PHASE 22: GO-TO-MARKET (GTM) STRATEGY

> "A great product launched quietly is a failed product. We must launch with maximum noise, targeting a very specific wedge."

### The Wedge: The "Recently Laid Off Tech Worker"
The tech industry is experiencing rolling layoffs. This creates a massive pool of highly skilled, highly motivated candidates who are desperate to stand out in a flooded market. This is our beachhead.

### 22.1 The Launch Sequence

**T-Minus 14 Days: The Teaser Campaign**
- No screenshots of the product.
- Tweet/LinkedIn posts from the founders: *"The PDF resume was invented in 1993. It's time to kill it. We've raised $5M to change how hiring starts."*
- Landing page captures emails for early access (The Waitlist).

**T-Minus 7 Days: The Influencer Seeding**
- We onboard our 10 chosen Tech/Design influencers (Phase 21).
- They quietly update their social bios to `1imp.io/firstname`.
- People naturally click the link and see the "Create yours" footer. Word-of-mouth begins in closed Discord/Slack communities.

**Launch Day (The Big Bang)**
- **Product Hunt Launch**: Coordinated push to hit #1 Product of the Day. Headline: "1IMP - The new standard for professional first impressions."
- **The "Burn Your Resume" Stunt**: A beautiful web app side-project `burnyourresume.com` that lets users upload a PDF resume, watch a gorgeous 3D animation of it burning, and instantly redirects them to the 1IMP onboarding flow.
- **LinkedIn Takeover**: The founding team and all investors simultaneously post their own 1IMP links with the story of why the company exists.

## PHASE 24: VERSION 2 ROADMAP

Once we achieve product-market fit (measured by sustained DAU/MAU and organic K-factor > 1.2), we expand rapidly.

### V1.5 (Months 3-6: Deepening the Consumer Wedge)
- **Video Pitch**: Asynchronous, 60-second video integration.
- **Custom Domains**: Connect `yourname.com` to the profile.
- **Analytics Dashboard V2**: Time-on-page heatmaps, device data.
- **AI Outreach Assistant**: Generates personalized LinkedIn messages for candidates to send to recruiters.

### V2.0 (Months 6-12: The B2B Gateway)
- **"Apply with 1IMP" Extension**: A Chrome extension that auto-fills Greenhouse/Lever application forms using the 1IMP JSON data, while pasting the `1imp.io` link in the "Website" field.
- **Recruiter View Enhancements**: Allow recruiters to "Save" or "Shortlist" candidates directly from the public page (creates a shadow account for the recruiter).
- **The Job Match Score**: Candidates can paste a job description, and the AI scores their 1IMP profile against it, suggesting edits before they apply.

## PHASE 25: THE LONG-TERM VISION (THE IPO/M&A THESIS)

> "We are not building a consumer app. We are building the data layer for the future of talent discovery."

### The 5-Year Master Plan

**Year 1: Win the Candidate**
Build the ultimate consumer tool for job seekers. Achieve viral scale. Become the status symbol for top-tier talent.

**Year 2: Trojan Horse the Recruiter**
Because candidates send our links everywhere, recruiters spend hours looking at 1IMP profiles. We build lightweight B2B tools (saved lists, team sharing) that recruiters adopt *because it's easier than their ATS*.

**Year 3: The Talent Marketplace**
With millions of active, highly-detailed candidate profiles (with better, fresher data than LinkedIn), we flip the switch. We allow paying B2B Enterprise customers to search the 1IMP database. We become a premium sourcing platform.

**Year 4: The ATS Disruption**
We launch 1IMP ATS. Because candidates prefer applying with 1IMP, and recruiters prefer reading 1IMP profiles, we provide the software that connects them directly. We replace Greenhouse and Lever for modern tech companies.

**Year 5: The Standard**
"Send me your resume" is replaced in the cultural lexicon by "Send me your impression." 

### The Exit Avenues
1. **Acquisition ($100M - $500M)**: LinkedIn acquires 1IMP to modernize its stale profile UX. Workday or Deel acquires 1IMP to build an unbreakable bridge between talent sourcing and HR software.
2. **IPO ($1B+)**: We successfully launch the B2B SaaS platform and disrupt the $3B+ Applicant Tracking System market, owning both the candidate data and the enterprise workflow.
