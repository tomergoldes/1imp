## Voyantis.ai Design Reference Analysis

Before defining the product, we studied the reference site you love. Here is what we extracted from Voyantis.ai:

**What Voyantis does exceptionally well (and we will reference):**
1. **Full-viewport hero with embedded video** — The product demo is the hero. There are no carousels or bullet points. The entire above-the-fold area IS the product demonstration. We will apply this: our hero shows a live, animated candidate card being generated.
2. **Minimal, restrained nav** — The navigation is sparse. Just "Products, Solutions, Resources, Company" and a single CTA. No clutter. Clean separation of structure.
3. **Alternating sticky sections** — As you scroll, content reveals in layers. It feels cinematic. We will use this mechanic in the landing page to progressively reveal the 3-step journey (Upload → Generate → Share).
4. **Social proof row** — Scrolling company logos. We will adapt this to a scrolling row of recruiter companies that have received 1IMP profiles ("Candidates using 1IMP have landed roles at:")
5. **Claim + Visual pairing** — Every headline claim is immediately followed by a visual/data proof. We will follow this rhythm.
6. **Typography size contrast** — Voyantis uses massive headline type against small body copy. This creates powerful visual hierarchy and forces prioritization.

# STEP 1: PRODUCT REDEFINITION

## What exactly are we building?

**We are building the first professional identity layer that exists between a candidate's raw experience and a recruiter's limited attention.**

The resume is a container. It holds information. But information without attention is invisible. 1IMP is the delivery mechanism that makes that information undeniable.

More precisely: **1IMP is an AI-powered system that transforms a candidate's unstructured professional history into a multi-format, beautifully designed, instantly shareable first impression — engineered to stop a recruiter in 3 seconds.**

## Why will users love it?

- For the first time, the product they send feels like it reflects their actual quality.
- The confidence of sending a `1imp.io/yourname` link instead of a PDF is visceral.
- When they get a notification "Someone at Stripe just viewed your impression," they feel seen.

## Why will recruiters adopt it?

- They won't "adopt" it consciously — that's the insight. Recruiters consume 1IMP links passively. A candidate sends it. The recruiter clicks it. It's beautiful, it's instant, it works. No form to fill, no account to create. The product bypasses their resistance entirely.

## What emotional problem are we solving?

**For candidates**: The crushing invisibility of being qualified but unnoticed. The 7-second rejection by an algorithm. The silence after 50 applications. 1IMP gives candidates their agency back. They stop being a PDF number and become a person.

**For recruiters**: The paralysis of 300 identical applications. The guilt of not being able to give every candidate proper attention. 1IMP makes their job feel humane again — they can understand a person in 10 seconds instead of 5 minutes.

## What is our category?

We are creating the category. The name: **Professional First Impression Platforms (PFIP)**.

We are not: a resume builder, a video generator, a job board, an ATS.

We are: the mechanism by which the world's talent makes its first move.

## What should users immediately understand?

In 5 seconds of seeing 1IMP: "This is the tool that will make recruiters actually notice me."

## Why would someone pay?

- To see which companies viewed their profile (the most powerful insight in job hunting)
- To send a first impression so polished it signals they are the top tier
- To get more interviews — that is the ultimate product promise

## Why now?

- AI makes high-quality narrative generation accessible for the first time
- The great tech layoffs of 2023-2025 have created millions of highly qualified, deeply frustrated candidates
- LinkedIn has stagnated. The UI is 10 years old. The UX is exhausting.
- Gen Z (the largest workforce cohort entering the market) expects rich media, AI, and design quality in their tools

## What makes us impossible to ignore?

- The public profile link is the marketing material
- Every candidate who shares their 1IMP link is our ad, our salesperson, and our testimonial simultaneously
- The experience of seeing a 1IMP profile for the first time is physically stunning. It is designed to cause a recruiter to pause.

# STEP 2: COMPLETE PRODUCT VISION

## Mission
Help every candidate create an unforgettable first impression.

## Vision
A world where no talented person is overlooked simply because they were never noticed. The global standard for professional first impressions.

---

## Product Principles

1. **Attention is the currency.** Every feature is measured by its ability to capture or hold a recruiter's attention.
2. **The candidate is the protagonist.** We serve them. The recruiter is served by serving the candidate.
3. **One question governs all decisions:** *Will this increase the candidate's chance of getting noticed?* If no, it is cut.
4. **Invisible by design.** The best technology disappears. The candidate's story is the product, not our technology.
5. **Confidence at every step.** Every interaction should make the candidate feel more ready, not more anxious.
6. **Scannability over depth.** Recruiters don't read. They scan, stop, then read. We design for the scan first.

---

## Product Philosophy

The resume contains the candidate's past. 1IMP reveals their potential. We are not digitizing the resume. We are humanizing it. The PDF resume answers: *"What have you done?"* 1IMP answers: *"Who are you, and why should I hire you right now?"*

---

## User Philosophy

Users are not job applicants. They are professionals managing a personal brand under time pressure and emotional stress. Every interaction must reduce anxiety, increase confidence, and give them a tangible action. Never make a user feel judged by our product.

---

## Recruiter Philosophy

Recruiters are not gatekeepers. They are overwhelmed professionals trying to do a nearly impossible job under absurd constraints. We are not fighting them. We are helping them. Every design decision for the public impression page is made from the recruiter's perspective: What do they need to see first? What can we remove? What must they be able to do in one click?

---

## AI Philosophy

The AI is an amplifier, not a ghostwriter. It surfaces what is already great about a candidate, structures it, and presents it with precision. It never invents. It never exaggerates. It translates. The AI should feel like the world's best career coach who has read everything about the candidate and knows exactly what to say to make a recruiter stop scrolling.

---

## Design Philosophy

We design for the reaction, not the feature. The question is never "Is this button in the right place?" The question is "What will a person feel when they see this?" Every screen must have a clear emotional intent: confident, clear, impressed, reassured, or excited. We are building a feeling, not a dashboard.

---

## Engineering Philosophy

Performance is a feature. The public impression page must load in under 1 second on mobile. A recruiter tapping a link in LinkedIn on their phone gets one chance. If it takes 3 seconds to load, they are gone. Every millisecond of page load time is a missed interview.

---

## Growth Philosophy

The product grows itself. Every candidate who shares their 1IMP link is our distribution channel. We do not spend on ads to acquire recruiters. Recruiters discover us because candidates send them links. We optimize the product for shareability above all else — including some features we'd personally love to build.

```
1. Signup (Google or Email)
2. Sees "Let's build your impression" — one giant upload zone

4. Watches the animated processing state ("Reading your story... Crafting your summary...")
```

# STEP 6: EVERY SCREEN DEFINED

## Screen 1: Landing Page (/)

- **Purpose**: Convert visitors to signups. The product is the hero.
- **Components**: Voyantis-inspired full-viewport hero, animated 3D candidate card, story section, 3-step how-it-works, live product demo, social proof logos, pricing section, sticky CTA bar
- **UX Goal**: Cause a "wow" reaction before the user reads a single word
- **Business Value**: Acquisition. Every visitor who signs up is the top of our funnel.
- **Primary Action**: "Create My Impression" (above fold + sticky)
- **Loading State**: Hero video/animation plays while page loads
- **Key Micro-Interaction**: Hovering the floating profile card causes it to rotate 3D slightly

## Screen 2: Signup / Login (/signup, /login)

- **Purpose**: Frictionless account creation
- **Components**: Google OAuth (primary, large), Email/Password (secondary, smaller), "Continue as Guest" (allows exploring editor before committing)
- **UX Goal**: First action should be "Continue with Google." No friction, no forms.
- **Loading State**: Spinner on Google OAuth button during handshake
- **Error State**: "Email already in use — Log in instead" with auto-redirect

## Screen 3: Onboarding Step 1 — Upload (/onboarding/upload)

- **Purpose**: Get resume content. This is the first commitment.
- **Components**: Enormous drag-and-drop zone (80% of viewport), "Upload Resume" label, file type indicators (PDF, DOCX, Max 5MB), Skip option below
- **UX Goal**: One action. No decisions. Just drop the file.
- **Loading State**: File icon animates into a progress arc. Text rotates: "Reading your history...", "Extracting your skills...", "Crafting your story..."
- **Error State**: "We couldn't read that file. Try a different PDF or paste your experience manually."
- **Success State**: Smooth fade transition to the Editor

- **Purpose**: The core product experience — the "Aha Moment" screen

- **Loading State**: Right pane shows a skeleton version of the profile card while data loads
- **Error State**: "AI generation failed. Your content is safe. Try regenerating." with retry button

# STEP 7: ALL AI CAPABILITIES

## Core (MVP)

### 1. Resume Extraction Engine
- **Input**: Raw PDF/DOCX text
- **Output**: Structured JSON (name, roles, companies, dates, education, skills, achievements)
- **Model**: GPT-4o-mini with JSON Schema enforcement
- **Constraint**: Never hallucinate — if data isn't present, return null

### 2. Professional Summary Generator
- **Input**: Structured profile JSON + Target Role
- **Output**: 2-3 sentence narrative. First person. Specific. Impact-led.
- **Model**: GPT-4o
- **Rules**: No buzzwords ("synergy", "results-driven"), no passive voice, no generic claims

### 3. Achievement Reformatter
- **Input**: Raw bullet points from resume
- **Output**: Impact-focused bullets following "Result + Method + Context" structure
- **Model**: GPT-4o-mini
- **Rule**: Preserve all facts. Only reframe the structure.

### 4. Quality Floor Evaluator
- **Input**: Full profile data
- **Output**: Pass/Fail + specific coaching message if fail
- **Logic**: Client-side rules engine (no AI needed for basic validation)

---

## V1.5 AI Features

### 5. AI Outreach Message Drafter
- **Input**: Candidate profile + Target company/recruiter name
- **Output**: Personalized 3-sentence LinkedIn message with a 1IMP link
- **Tone**: Professional, human, direct

### 6. Career Story Generator
- **Input**: Full profile + Career goals
- **Output**: A paragraph-length first-person narrative connecting past to future
- **Use**: Sits below the Summary on the impression page. Optional section.

### 7. LinkedIn Optimization Advisor

# STEP 8: THE MVP

## What makes the cut

The MVP must validate one hypothesis: **"Candidates who share a 1IMP profile get more recruiter responses than those who send a PDF."**

To validate this, we need exactly and only:

| Feature | Why It's MVP |
|---------|-------------|
| Resume Parsing + AI Extraction | Without this, there's no product |
| AI Professional Summary | This is the single highest-value output |
| Split-Screen Editor | Creates the "Aha Moment" — our virality trigger |
| Public Impression Page | The delivery mechanism for value |
| Shareable Link + Open Graph Cards | The viral growth loop |
| PDF Download on Profile | Recruiter trust + ATS compatibility |
| Basic Analytics (view count) | Proves the product is working |
| Google Auth | Frictionless signup |
| Stripe Billing (Free + Pro) | Revenue validation |

## What doesn't make the MVP cut (and why)

| Cut Feature | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Video Pitch | High complexity, low early adoption, requires separate infra |
| LinkedIn Import | Nice-to-have, not essential when PDF works |
| Custom Domains | Adds complexity; `username.1imp.io` works fine for V1 |
| Multiple Profiles | Adds cognitive load before users understand core value |
| AI Outreach Drafter | Great feature but not required for the viral loop to work |
| B2B Recruiter Dashboard | Wrong sequence — must win candidate side first |
